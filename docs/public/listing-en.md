## Name

NetKit

## Short description

Wi-Fi, LAN, SSH and Wake-on-LAN for networks you own or have permission to use.

## Full description

Keep your network tools at hand. NetKit brings connection details, Wi-Fi analysis, device discovery, SSH and Wake-on-LAN into one app. Use it only on networks you own or have permission to manage.

Connection overview
Follow Wi-Fi and mobile signal strength, gateway, internet and DNS latency, public IP and provider details. Expand live charts and explore changes over time. Public IP lookup can be disabled.

Wi-Fi and devices
Inspect nearby access points, channels, bands and security. Find devices on your Wi-Fi network and available details about their services. Automatic discovery requires your consent for that network.

Network tools
Use ping, traceroute, TCP port tests and DNS to diagnose connections. Save or share results.

SSH and Wake-on-LAN
Connect to your own servers using SSH profiles, passwords or keys. NetKit checks server keys and protects saved SSH secrets with encryption backed by Android Keystore. Wake devices using Wake-on-LAN and import or export your device list.

Connection speed
Measure download, upload and latency with LibreSpeed or M-Lab NDT7. Choose the rychlost.zelina.eu preset or your own HTTPS server. Tests can consume large amounts of data. M-Lab publishes results including your public IP; a notice appears before the first test.

No account or advertising
NetKit does not send usage analytics to its author. Network features communicate with selected devices and services; see the privacy policy for details. History and profiles are stored in the app, while exports go to the storage you choose.

The interface is available in Czech and English. Wi-Fi and mobile network details depend on your device and granted permissions. NetKit is not a tool for capturing other users' traffic.
