IEEE OUI Public Listing

NetKit shows the vendor of a Wi-Fi access point from an offline copy of the IEEE Registration
Authority public listings MA-L (24-bit OUI), MA-M (28-bit) and MA-S (36-bit). Only the assigned
prefix and the organization name are included; organization addresses are not. The copy was
downloaded on 2026-11-02 from:

- https://standards-oui.ieee.org/oui/oui.csv
- https://standards-oui.ieee.org/oui28/mam.csv
- https://standards-oui.ieee.org/oui36/oui36.csv

Statement attributed to IEEE: "IEEE does not assert any copyright in the OUI Public Listing or attempt to restrict
distribution of the listing in any way. The IEEE Registration Authority does, however, strongly
encourage those who use the list to obtain it directly from IEEE (and to update it on a regular
basis) to ensure the integrity of the information."

Source of the statement: Debian package ieee-data, copyright file (2014),
https://metadata.ftp-master.debian.org/changelogs/main/i/ieee-data/ieee-data_20141019.1_copyright
(checked 2026-11-02). Debian classifies these files as "License: other" with the note "Public
Domain"; that is Debian's classification, not a statement by IEEE. An IEEE page carrying this
statement was not found on 2026-11-02 (the IEEE Registration Authority FAQ does not contain it).

The listing is not part of the NetKit source code licence. Names are shown as published by IEEE
(whitespace and invisible characters normalized); NetKit does not verify them, and the vendor of
an access point may differ from the owner of its OUI.
